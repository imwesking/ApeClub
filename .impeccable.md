## Design Context

### Users
General users discovering ApeBond / DeFi for the first time. Not experienced crypto natives — people who may not know what "True-Yield", "Governance", or "Ape2Earn" mean and need the landing page to make them feel welcome and intrigued enough to join.

### Brand Personality
Accessible and exclusive. The product should feel premium and desirable without being intimidating. It's a club — welcoming to newcomers but clearly worth belonging to.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode cyberpunk-adjacent with the NaNa character as a central visual anchor. Purple/pink/cyan/gold palette already established. Poppins as main typeface. The custom 911Porscha font (available in /fonts/) is currently unused.

### Design Principles
1. **Welcome first**: The first-timer should never feel lost — copy and hierarchy must guide them clearly.
2. **Exclusivity without elitism**: Premium feel without alienating someone who just found out what DeFi is.
3. **NaNa as personality**: The character is the emotional center — the design should amplify her, not compete with her.
4. **Earned complexity**: DeFi jargon (True-Yield, Governance, Ape2Earn) should be introduced gently, not assumed.
5. **One clear action**: The CTA (Join/Connect) should be unmissable at every scroll point.
